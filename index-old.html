Our Story

Built With Purpose,
Driven by Care

"We believe that where you live should never determine the quality of healthcare you receive."

— Founder, Global Prime Pathology

Global Prime Pathology was born from a deeply personal observation — that residents of Himmatnagar and surrounding areas often had to travel to Ahmedabad or other metros for reliable diagnostic tests. This was not just inconvenient; it delayed treatment and added unnecessary stress to patients already dealing with health concerns.

Our founders set out to change this. With years of experience in medical diagnostics and a genuine passion for patient welfare, they established Global Prime Pathology with one clear goal: to bring the best of metro-level diagnostic infrastructure to the heart of Himmatnagar.

We invested in high-end automated analyzers, built a team of qualified and caring professionals, and designed a patient experience that reflects the respect every person deserves. From the moment a patient walks through our door (or we walk through theirs, for home collection), we are focused on one thing — delivering results that are accurate, timely, and meaningful.

Today, Global Prime Pathology proudly serves thousands of patients, works alongside local doctors and clinics, and continues to grow — not just in tests and technology, but in the trust we have earned from our community. This is our purpose, and it guides everything we do.

Diagnostic Services

Our Complete Range
of Tests

From everyday health checks to specialised diagnostic panels — we cover every aspect of your diagnostic needs with precision and care.

Routine Tests
Your everyday health baseline

Routine tests are the foundation of preventive healthcare. These tests help establish a healthy baseline, monitor existing conditions, and catch early warning signs before they become serious health concerns.

Whether prescribed by your doctor or taken as part of a regular health check, our routine tests deliver precise results quickly and affordably.

Who should get this done?

Everyone — especially annually for health monitoring, or as advised by your doctor.

Common Tests Include
Complete Blood Count (CBC)
Blood Sugar (Fasting & PP)
Urine Routine Analysis
Lipid Profile
HbA1c
ESR
Stool Routine
Biochemistry
Deep insight into organ function

Biochemistry tests measure the chemical composition of your blood and body fluids. These tests provide critical information about how well your organs — especially the liver, kidneys, and pancreas — are functioning.

Using fully automated biochemistry analyzers, we ensure consistent, accurate results that doctors can rely on for diagnosis and treatment planning.

Who should get this done?

Patients with diabetes, liver concerns, kidney issues, or as part of comprehensive health packages.

Common Tests Include
Liver Function Test (LFT)
Kidney Function Test (KFT)
Serum Electrolytes
SGOT / SGPT
Bilirubin (Total & Direct)
Serum Creatinine
Uric Acid
Hematology
Understanding your blood health

Hematology is the study of blood — its cells, composition, and disorders. These tests help detect and monitor conditions like anaemia, infections, clotting disorders, and more serious blood conditions.

Our fully automated haematology analyzers, combined with expert review, deliver results that are fast, reliable, and clinically meaningful.

Who should get this done?

Anyone experiencing fatigue, anaemia, frequent infections, or as part of routine check-ups.

Common Tests Include
Complete Blood Count (CBC)
Peripheral Blood Smear
Erythrocyte Sedimentation Rate (ESR)
Reticulocyte Count
Platelet Count
Prothrombin Time (PT)
APTT
Microbiology
Identifying infections precisely

Microbiology tests identify the microorganisms (bacteria, fungi, viruses) causing an infection. Culture and sensitivity tests go a step further — they determine which antibiotic will work best, helping doctors prescribe the most effective treatment.

This precision is crucial in avoiding unnecessary medication and reducing antibiotic resistance.

Who should get this done?

Patients with persistent infections, UTIs, respiratory issues, or before starting antibiotic treatment.

Common Tests Include
Urine Culture & Sensitivity
Blood Culture
Throat Swab Culture
Sputum Culture
Stool Culture
Wound / Pus Culture
Immunology & Serology
Your immune system decoded

Immunology and serology tests evaluate your immune system's response and detect specific antibodies or antigens related to infections, autoimmune conditions, and allergies.

From detecting Dengue and Typhoid to screening for Hepatitis and HIV, these tests play a vital role in accurate diagnosis and patient safety.

Who should get this done?

Patients with suspected infections, allergies, autoimmune conditions, or routine health screening.

Common Tests Include
Widal Test (Typhoid)
Dengue NS1 / IgM / IgG
HBsAg (Hepatitis B)
Anti-HCV (Hepatitis C)
HIV 1 & 2 Screening
RA Factor
ASO Titre
Hormonal Assays
Uncover the invisible signals

Hormones regulate almost every function in your body — from metabolism and mood to reproductive health and growth. Hormonal assays measure the levels of these critical chemical messengers to diagnose imbalances, thyroid disorders, reproductive issues, and more.

We use modern immunoassay technology for highly sensitive and specific hormonal testing.

Who should get this done?

Patients experiencing fatigue, weight changes, fertility issues, irregular periods, or thyroid symptoms.

Common Tests Include
TSH, T3, T4 (Thyroid)
FSH, LH, Prolactin
Testosterone
Oestradiol (E2)
Progesterone
Cortisol
Insulin (Fasting)

Call us and our team will help you understand what's right for your health situation.

How We Work

Precision at Every Step

From the moment a sample arrives in our lab to the moment your report is delivered — quality is never compromised.

Fully Automated Analyzers

Our lab is equipped with high-throughput automated analyzers for biochemistry, hematology, and immunology. Automation reduces human error and ensures consistent, reproducible results across every test, every time.

Rigorous Quality Control

We run quality control checks at every analytical run. Internal controls (IQC) are performed daily, and we participate in External Quality Assurance Schemes (EQAS) to benchmark our performance against national and international standards.

Regular Calibration

All instruments are calibrated regularly using traceable standards. Calibration records are maintained and reviewed to ensure our equipment performs within tight tolerance limits at all times.

Traceability of Results

Every sample is uniquely barcoded and tracked through our Laboratory Information System (LIS) from collection to reporting. This eliminates mix-ups and creates a full audit trail for every result.

Sample Integrity

Samples are collected, stored, and transported under controlled conditions to preserve their integrity. Temperature-controlled storage and timely processing ensure that the sample tested is truly representative of what was collected.

Expert Oversight

Our qualified pathologists review critical and abnormal results before they are released. This expert oversight adds an additional layer of verification, ensuring that no clinically significant finding goes unnoticed or unreported.

Our Quality Promise

We are committed to delivering results with a coefficient of variation that meets or exceeds international laboratory standards. When you receive a result from Global Prime Pathology, it is a result you and your doctor can act on with confidence.
